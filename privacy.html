ONE CAST

Privacy policy · last updated October 26, 2026

One Cast is a free fishing game you can play at onecast.github.io and in the One Cast iPhone app. This page explains what the game stores about you and why. Questions: onecastgame@gmail.com

WHAT THE GAME STORES

  • Your angler name and #tag. You pick the name. Other players see it on leaderboards, legends, World News, friends lists and profiles, so please don't use your real name.
  • A player ID and a secret key that the game makes up for you. They are saved on your device so you stay logged in, and together they are your save code.
  • Your game progress: fish, coins, bait, gear, boats, clothes, journal, achievements, daily challenges, friends, trades, reactions, and any reports or lake suggestions you send.
  • A password, only if you set one. It is stored scrambled (hashed), so nobody can read it, including us.
  • When you last played, so friends can see if you are online.

WHAT IT DOES NOT COLLECT

No email address, real name, phone number, location, contacts, photos or advertising ID. There are no ads and no tracking, and we never sell or share your data for advertising.

WHERE IT IS KEPT

  • Game data is stored with our database provider, Supabase.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your device connects to Google to download them, and Google can see your IP address when it does.
  • In the iPhone app, bite alerts are scheduled on your phone itself. Nothing extra is sent anywhere to make them work.

KIDS

One Cast is made for everyone. We don't ask for or knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The game only stores the game data listed above. A parent who wants a player's data removed can delete the account in the game or email us.

DELETING YOUR DATA

In the game, open MENU, then SAVE YOUR GAME, then DELETE MY ACCOUNT. Your angler, fish, coins, boats, friends and trades are deleted right away, and any legend you own goes back into the water. You can also email onecastgame@gmail.com with your name#tag and we will delete it for you.

Otherwise we keep your game data for as long as your account exists.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
