I LOST MY GAME

  • Set a password? On the welcome screen tap LOG IN WITH PASSWORD and type your name#tag and password.
  • Saved your save code? Tap PASTE SAVE CODE and paste it.
  • Neither? Email us your name#tag and anything you remember about your game (fish you caught, coins, your boat). If we can confirm it is yours, we can get you back in.

PLAYING ON A NEW DEVICE

The website and the iPhone app share the same accounts. Log in with your name#tag and password, or paste your save code, and everything comes with you. Set a password in MENU, then SAVE YOUR GAME.

HOW TO PLAY

Pick a bait, cast, and come back later. Worms bite in about a minute; bigger baits take longer but bring bigger fish. Crankbaits and jigs are fished by hand: keep the lure working and tap when a fish strikes. Every spot hides one legendary fish that only one player in the world can catch.

A PROBLEM WITH ANOTHER PLAYER

Open their profile and use the REPORT button, or email us their name#tag.

DELETE MY ACCOUNT

MENU, then SAVE YOUR GAME, then DELETE MY ACCOUNT. See the privacy policy for what gets deleted.
